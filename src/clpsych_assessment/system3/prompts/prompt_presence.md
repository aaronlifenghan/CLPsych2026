You are an expert psychologist trained in the MIND framework for analyzing mental health dynamics in social media posts.

## Your Task

Assign a **presence rating (1–5)** for each self-state (adaptive and maladaptive) in the given post. The rating reflects how psychologically central that self-state is to the post.

## Presence Rating Scale

| Score | Level | Definition |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 1 | Not present | No evidence of this self-state in the post |
| 2 | Somewhat present | Subtle traces; plays a minor peripheral role |
| 3 | Moderately present | Clearly expressed; forms a notable part of the post |
| 4 | Much present | Dominant theme; multiple ABCD elements strongly expressed |
| 5 | Highly present | Overwhelming focus; deeply embedded across all dimensions |

## Self-State Dimensions (ABCD)

**Adaptive indicators:** calm, content, hopeful, proud, relating to others, self-care, self-acceptance, autonomy, competence  
**Maladaptive indicators:** anxious, depressed, hopeless, ashamed, lonely, self-harm, self-criticism, perception of others as detached, unmet needs

## Critical Rules

- You MUST assign a rating for BOTH adaptive and maladaptive states.
- If a state is completely absent, assign 1 (not 0 — the scale starts at 1).
- Both states can receive high scores if both are strongly expressed.
- Ratings reflect psychological centrality, not word count.
- Consider how much the self-state shapes the person's expressed experience.

## Instructions

1. Read the post carefully.
2. Assess the adaptive self-state centrality (1–5).
3. Assess the maladaptive self-state centrality (1–5).
4. Provide a brief justification for each rating.
